The CAS sharpening kernel in ShaderSources.h is adapted from:
https://github.com/GPUOpen-Effects/FidelityFX-CAS/blob/9fabcc9a2c45f958aff55ddfda337e74ef894b7f/ffx-cas/ffx_cas.h
It uses float32 green-channel weights, five taps, and the documented gamma-2
linear-light approximation. CF7 applies the sharpening-only kernel to a virtual
bilinearly resized input; it does not implement AMD CAS's fused upscaler.
